# TrendCraft: 2-minute demo script

**Before recording**

- Run the whole flow once so the news and angles are cached (1 hour), and render one reel ahead of time as a backup.
- Have a few kits in the Library, with at least one marked Posted, so the chatbot has something to answer from.
- Keep 2 or 3 post screenshots ready in an easy-to-find folder.
- Check that the page shows no "Demo mode" note. If you do record with sample data, leave the note visible and say so aloud.
- Trim the waits on model calls in editing, but keep a few seconds of the newsroom screen: it's worth showing.
- Replace the bracketed lines with what's actually on screen. Don't read out a headline, score or claim you can't see.

| Time | On screen | Say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:12 | Home page | "If you create content about the news, you spend hours each day working out what's worth covering, writing it up, and hoping you got the facts right. TrendCraft does all of that, with sources." |
| 0:12–0:25 | Click **Finance**, then **Find what's trending**. Show the newsroom screen | "I pick my niche. While I wait, I can see the real stories being read. Meta's Muse Spark model groups them into angles." |
| 0:25–0:38 | Angle cards. Point at a score and a why-now line | "These are angles, not headlines, scored for freshness, emotional pull and how easy they are to explain. This one scores [score] because [read the why-now line]." |
| 0:38–0:50 | Choose **Reel**, upload screenshots under **Match my style** | "I want this to feel like posts I already like. Muse Spark reads their tone, layout and colours. Here it found [read the style line]." |
| 0:50–1:08 | Click **Build kit**. Show the writers' room, then hooks, caption tabs and the cover | "A few seconds later I have three hooks, captions in three tones, a five-beat script and hashtags. The cover comes from Muse Image, in my style." |
| 1:08–1:22 | Point at the fact-check result, then **Sources** | "Muse Wonder checks every claim against the source articles. [If flagged: It caught this one, so I'd fix it before posting. If clear: Nothing was flagged this time, and I can still check the sources myself.]" |
| 1:22–1:38 | Click **Create reel**. Show scenes appearing, then cut to the finished video | "One click turns the script into a reel. Muse Image paints each scene, and SAM finds the subject so the camera moves to it and the captions stay out of its way. I add my own voice or sound on top." |
| 1:38–1:50 | Open **Library**. Change a status to **Posted**. Click **Download everything** | "Everything is saved: the reel, cover, captions and script, each kit marked new, posted or archived." |
| 1:50–2:00 | Open **Ask my content**, ask "What haven't I posted yet?" | "And I can just ask my content what to post next. That's TrendCraft, built on Meta's Muse models." |

**Before you publish the video**

- If you show the voice button, make sure you've tested it with your microphone first.
- Don't call the reel "AI-generated video". The scenes are AI images, and the video is assembled from them.
